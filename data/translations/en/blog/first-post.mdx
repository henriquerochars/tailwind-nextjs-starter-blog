I wanted to create this blog to share a little of what I learn in my day-to-day work, and also to practice writing, as if it were a technical diary.

I plan to separate diary posts (with the diary tag) from technical articles, which will have a tag for the technology related to the post.
I also intend to publish the more technical posts in English on [dev.to](dev.to).

I was inspired by my friend [Caio Fuzzato](https://caiofuzatto.com.br/), who created a very similar blog with the same goal.

I currently work as a senior full-stack developer with the NextJS and NestJS frameworks, so I am very focused on JavaScript and TypeScript technologies. There will be plenty of content about the JS world, but I also plan to explore other technologies.

See you later 👋 :D
